    Why exact matching

    Silent partial funding is the failure mode this policy exists to prevent. A gateway that settles R 1 000 against a R 1 499 escrow must never mark the escrow funded — the seller would ship goods against money that was never collected.

    fundSuccessful status + exact amount
    ignoreNon-successful status, acknowledged with no state change
